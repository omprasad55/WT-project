01 / ABOUT

More than
just classrooms.

Education is not limited to textbooks. We encourage students to question, experiment and discover their own potential.

NIST College is an institution dedicated to providing quality education with a balance of theory and practical learning. Students are encouraged to develop technical knowledge, creativity, communication skills and confidence.

Our Mission

To provide students with quality education, practical skills and an environment that encourages learning and innovation.

Our Vision

To develop responsible, skilled and creative graduates who can contribute positively to society.
